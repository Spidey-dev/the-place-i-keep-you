Chapter three · The day we met

The day the screen
became real.

We had planned to meet, then you changed your mind more than once. You were getting cold feet. I kept believing the moment would happen.

The trip

I came anyway.

A friend’s mother was being buried in a village close to your university. I attended the burial, then came to the community where you school and stayed two nights in a hotel.

That morning

I went looking for the right place.

You wanted somewhere quiet and private. So I spent the early hours scouting until I found an eatery close to the hotel with a little room marked VIP.

Then you walked in

Everything else disappeared.

The moment I saw you walking toward the entrance, I was dazzled. It felt like the whole world froze and nothing else mattered for a while.

Our first date

Two shy people. One table.

I ordered snacks and rice for us. We were both so shy we could barely eat. We talked, watched the time move, and somehow made a memory I still carry.
