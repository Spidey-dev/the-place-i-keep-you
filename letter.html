Chapter five · A letter

My Own Babe,
this one is for you.

Stella, I still think about how this story began.

I sent you a friend request years ago. I liked your picture the first time I saw your profile. You didn't reply. And then, just days before Valentine’s in February 2026, you finally did.

We talked almost every day. We planned to meet. You changed your mind. You got cold feet.

And somehow, after all of that, there I was — sitting across from you in that quiet VIP room, looking at the woman I had spent so long only knowing through a screen.

The moment you walked through that entrance, something inside me went quiet.

I looked at you and felt like my search was over.

I know life will not always be easy. But I know what I want: to keep choosing you, to keep building with you, and to keep making room for you in every version of the future I imagine.

When you call me “My Mr Marvis,” I hear more than a nickname. I hear a promise of the life I hope we are building.

And when I call you “My Own Babe,” I mean it with my whole heart.

Forever yours,
My Mr Marvis ♡
