A letter

Things I don't
always say.

I don't know if words ever really manage to explain what someone means to you.

But I want you to know this: you matter to me in the quiet places too. Not only when we're laughing, not only when everything is easy, but in the ordinary days and the moments between them.

I love the person you are, the person you're becoming, and the little pieces of you that you don't think anyone notices.

Whenever you doubt yourself, remember there is someone looking at you thinking: I'm really lucky.

— Marvis
