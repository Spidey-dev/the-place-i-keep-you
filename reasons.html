Chapter two · The first hello

I noticed you
before you noticed me.

Years ago, somewhere inside Facebook, I sent you a friend request. I liked your picture the first time I saw your profile. And then... nothing.

Then

You didn't reply.

I had no idea that a request sitting quietly for years would eventually become the beginning of my favourite story.

A few days before Valentine’s · February 2026

Then you finally did.

I remember how excited I was when your reply finally came. Suddenly, the person I had once only seen on a screen was talking to me.
